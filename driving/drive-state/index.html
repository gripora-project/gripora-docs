    Drive State¶

    A car's turn response to a steering angle depends on its speed and its direction of travel. Driving backwards the response changes sign, so a gyro correction tuned going forward would steer the wrong way and become positive feedback. Near a standstill the car doesn't rotate at all, however far the wheels are turned, so a gyro left fully on would wind up and slam the steering to full lock.

    The drive state is how the controller handles this. It works out which way the car is travelling and how fast, and the steering loop uses that to:

    • turn the gyro correction round in reverse. The steering wheel keeps meaning "wheel angle" in both directions; only the gyro feedback is reversed;
    • fade the gyro correction out at low speed, so at a standstill the steering follows the wheel alone;
    • reduce, or switch off, the correction in reverse;
    • clear the steering I-term whenever the travel direction changes.

    The settings are on the Motors tab, in the Drive panel, which also shows the drive state live. The drive state is also used by ABS to know when the ESC is braking.

    ESC behaviour¶

    RPM sensors report how fast the motor turns, not which way. The direction comes from what the controller tells the ESC, so it needs to know what your ESC does with a command below neutral:

    Drive panel CLI drive_esc_mode Below neutral the ESC...
    Automatic AUTO (default) Picked from the ESC protocol: Reverse directly with DShot (AM32's default), Brake, then reverse otherwise (PWM and the rest)
    Brake, then reverse FWD_BRK_REV Brakes after driving forward. Back to neutral, then below neutral again, and it reverses. Most car ESCs, and AM32 with "RC car reverse" on
    Reverse directly FWD_REV Reverses straight away; while the car still rolls forward it brakes or coasts first. AM32's default bidirectional mode
    Forward and brake only FWD_BRK Only ever brakes. The car never reverses

    An AM32 with "RC car reverse" turned on behaves like most car ESCs and needs Brake, then reverse set by hand; Automatic would pick Reverse directly for it because it runs DShot.

    If you aren't sure which your ESC does, the setup wizard's ESC step has a test: with the wheels off the ground, hold forward, let go, then hold reverse straight away. If the wheels only stop, and turn backwards only after you let go and hold reverse again, it is Brake, then reverse.

    Speed from motor RPM¶

    With a motor RPM source, the controller turns motor RPM into the car's speed:

    speed = motor RPM / gear ratio x tyre circumference
    • RPM sources: bidirectional DShot RPM telemetry, ESC telemetry that reports RPM, or an RPM (frequency) sensor input. Set the motor's pole count so electrical RPM becomes motor RPM. See Motors. DShot150 can't carry bidirectional DShot RPM.
    • Gear ratio: motor turns per wheel turn, the final drive ratio. The Drive panel shows it as motor : wheel, so for a 10.4 : 1 final drive enter 104 : 10. In the CLI the two numbers are the other way round, wheel first: motor1_gear_ratio = 10,104. The default is 1 : 1. The RPM filter uses it too, and it applies after a save and restart.
    • Tyre diameter: outside diameter of the driven tyres, drive_wheel_diameter, 20-300 mm, default 100 mm.
    • Two motors: motor 1's speed is used, averaged with motor 2's when motor 2 has an RPM source too. Each has its own gear ratio (motor1_gear_ratio, motor2_gear_ratio).

    The car counts as stopped once its speed has been below 1 km/h for 0.1 s. The same speed is used by speed attenuation on the Profiles tab, in place of GPS speed.

    Wheel speed is what is measured, not ground speed: wheelspin and locked wheels show as faster or slower than the car is really going.

    Travel direction¶

    With RPM, the direction only changes at zero speed: when the car is stopped, or as the wheels move off from (near) zero. Braking while rolling forward is never taken for reverse. Wheels speeding up from zero under a command below neutral are taken as reverse (in every mode that can reverse, since a brake can't set them turning), and under a command above neutral as forward. So a reversal the model didn't expect, such as an ESC reversing at its own threshold, is still caught.

    Without RPM, the direction follows the command straight away:

    • forward when the ESC is driving forward, reverse when the ESC model says it reverses. Braking and neutral keep the last direction;
    • the ESC may brake or coast for a while before the car really changes direction, so after each change the gyro correction drops to zero and fades back in over 1 second.

    Reverse directly without RPM

    With Reverse directly (FWD_REV, which is also what Automatic picks on DShot) and no RPM source, the controller can't tell braking from reversing: any command below neutral is taken as reverse, even while the car is still rolling forward on the brake. The gyro correction is then turned round (fading in from zero over 1 s) and ABS doesn't run. Use an RPM source, or an ESC set to brake, then reverse.

    With Brake, then reverse and no RPM, the controller expects reverse once the throttle has been at neutral for at least 50 ms after a brake and then goes below neutral again. If your ESC needs a longer pause, or a full stop, before it reverses, the controller will think it is reversing early. An RPM source avoids the guess.

    Steering gyro correction¶

    The gyro's part of the steering (P, I and D) is scaled by three factors. The feedforward (F and B), the part that comes straight from the stick, is never scaled.

    1. Speed (needs motor RPM). No correction below Gyro starts at (drive_gyro_min_speed, default 2.0 km/h), rising to full at Gyro fully on at (drive_gyro_full_speed, default 6.0 km/h). Without RPM this factor is always full.
    2. Reverse. While reversing the correction is Reverse gyro gain (drive_gyro_reverse_gain, default 50%) of the forward correction, or none with Gyro in reverse off (drive_gyro_reverse = OFF).
    3. Direction change fade (without RPM only): zero right after a change of direction, back to full after 1 s.

    The steering I-term is cleared on every change of travel direction, since it was built up for the other direction.

    While disarmed the car is on the bench: the direction is held at forward, the speed fade doesn't apply, and the I-term is held at zero. That is why turning the car by hand on the bench shows the gyro correcting, which the setup wizard's gyro direction check relies on. Every arming starts from neutral and forward.

    Gyro direction is set once, going forward

    The reversal for reverse is automatic. If the gyro corrects the wrong way, that is a forward setting: see Car Mixer and Channels.

    Settings¶

    Setting Drive panel Range Default
    drive_esc_mode ESC behaviour AUTO, FWD_BRK_REV, FWD_REV, FWD_BRK AUTO
    drive_wheel_diameter Tyre diameter 20-300 mm 100
    motor1_gear_ratio, motor2_gear_ratio Gear ratio (shown motor : wheel) two numbers, wheel turns, motor turns 1,1
    drive_gyro_min_speed Gyro starts at 10-1999, in 0.1 km/h 20 (2.0 km/h)
    drive_gyro_full_speed Gyro fully on at always above the start speed, up to 2000 (200 km/h) 60 (6.0 km/h)
    drive_gyro_reverse Gyro in reverse OFF, ON ON
    drive_gyro_reverse_gain Reverse gyro gain 0-100 % 50

    These are global settings, not per profile. The start speed can't go below 1.0 km/h, the speed at which the direction may flip, so the correction is always faded out before the direction changes. The controller refuses a Drive panel save as a whole if a value is out of range or the full speed isn't above the start speed.

    Checking it¶

    The Drive panel's Live box shows the ESC behaviour in use (never Automatic: it shows what Automatic picked), the ESC state (Neutral, Forward, Braking, Reverse), the direction, the speed and the gyro correction in percent.

    For logs, set debug_mode = DRIVE and record a Blackbox log. The debug fields are: throttle command x1000, ESC state (0 neutral, 1 forward, 2 brake, 3 reverse), direction, speed x10 (km/h), speed known, correction scale x1000, motor 1 RPM, stopped.

    Source: src/main/flight/drive.c in the firmware.
