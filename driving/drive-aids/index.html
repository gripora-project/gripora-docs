    Drive Aids¶

    The drive aids shape the throttle and brake you give before they reach the ESC. There are three:

    • ABS pulses the brake when you brake hard, so the wheels keep turning and the car keeps steering instead of locking up and sliding.
    • Traction control limits how fast the forward throttle may rise, so the wheels don't spin up when you pull the trigger hard.
    • Launch control caps the throttle for a standing start from a switch, then ramps it up to full, so the car leaves the line without wheelspin.

    All are off by default. They work from the throttle, steering and time only: no wheel speed, slip or acceleration sensor is read. Speed-based steering and sensor-based aids are planned as separate modes.

    They are set on the Profiles tab, in the Drive aids panel, from the Ethos Lua suite's ABS, Traction and Launch pages, the EdgeTX Drive Aids page, or in the CLI (drive_abs_*, drive_tc_*, drive_lc_*).

    Racing rules

    Sensor-based driving aids are banned in sanctioned racing, and class rules may ban any electronic driving aid. Check the rules before racing with the drive aids, or the steering gyro, switched on.

    When the aids work¶

    The aids only ever move the command towards neutral, never past it, and never into the opposite direction. They pass the command through unchanged:

    • while disarmed;
    • in failsafe (the failsafe throttle replaces their output, so it is never shaped);
    • while the DRIVE AIDS OFF mode is on. Put it on a switch on the Modes tab to turn all the aids off from the transmitter. The steering gyro keeps working.

    GYRO OFF does not turn the drive aids off: it bypasses only the steering gyro.

    The aids act on the Stabilized Throttle mixer input, which is what the car mixer preset sends to the ESC. A mixer rule that feeds an ESC straight from the RC throttle channel bypasses them. See Car Mixer and Channels.

    Per PID profile¶

    The drive aids settings belong to the PID profile, like the speed attenuation settings: each of the six profiles has its own, and switching profile (from the Profiles tab, an adjustment or the radio) switches them with the tune. That makes a profile a track or surface preset. Copying a profile, or resetting one, includes its drive aids settings.

    ABS¶

    ABS works only while the ESC is braking a car that is still rolling: the controller's drive state must have the ESC in its brake state, and, if there is an RPM source, the car must not show as stopped. It never works in reverse or on forward throttle.

    Past the trigger the brake is pulsed. Each pulse lasts one cycle (1 / pulse rate):

    1. Full brake (the brake you give) for half the cycle plus the hold, but never more than 85 % of the cycle.
    2. Release: the brake eases to the release brake for the rest of the cycle, a percentage of the brake you give.

    The release brake never drops below 10 % brake. Most car ESCs brake first and reverse after the throttle has been back at neutral: if ABS released the brake to neutral, the ESC would take the next pulse as a request to reverse. Keeping at least 10 % brake keeps the command on the brake side of neutral, so the ESC (and the controller's drive state) stay in brake through the whole stop. If your brake is lighter than 10 %, the release keeps the brake you give.

    Below the trigger the brake goes to the ESC unchanged, and the pulse cycle starts again from the beginning the next time the brake passes the trigger or after any forward throttle.

    With the defaults (10 pulses per second, 20 ms hold, 40 % release brake) a full brake is 70 ms of full brake, then 30 ms at 40 % brake, ten times a second. The Drive aids panel shows this preview for your settings.

    Modes¶

    Panel CLI drive_abs_mode What it does
    Off OFF (default) The brake goes to the ESC as you give it
    Fixed STATIC Past the trigger the brake pulses at a fixed rate, release brake and hold
    Follows brake depth DYNAMIC Like Fixed, but the harder you brake (from the trigger to full brake), the more the pulse rate moves towards the full-brake rate, the release brake eases towards 60 % of its setting, and the hold moves towards the full-brake hold

    Steering¶

    Two settings change the pulses with steering lock, measured from the steering wheel (not the servo): none with the wheel centred, full at full lock.

    • Extra pulse rate adds pulses per second at full lock, less with less steering.
    • Hold (with steering) moves the hold towards this value as the steering approaches full lock.

    The pulse rate never goes above 40 pulses per second, whatever is added. Where a full-brake hold (Follows brake depth) and a steering hold both apply, the shorter hold wins; neither can lengthen the hold beyond the base Hold.

    ESC setups ABS can't run on¶

    ABS needs to know the ESC is braking, not reversing:

    • Reverse directly (drive_esc_mode = FWD_REV) without an RPM source: every command below neutral is taken as reverse, so ABS never runs. Automatic picks Reverse directly on DShot, so this includes a DShot ESC without RPM telemetry. The Drive aids panel warns about this. Fit an RPM source (bidirectional DShot, ESC telemetry or an RPM sensor), or set the ESC to brake, then reverse if it supports it, and set the Drive panel to match.
    • With an RPM source, ABS stops once the car is stopped (below 1 km/h for 0.1 s).
    • Forward and brake only and Brake, then reverse ESCs work with or without RPM.

    ABS settings¶

    Panel CLI Range Default
    Mode drive_abs_mode OFF, STATIC, DYNAMIC OFF
    Trigger drive_abs_trigger 5-100 % of full brake 30
    Pulse rate drive_abs_freq 2-33 pulses per second (Hz) 10
    Release brake drive_abs_floor 0-100 % of your brake (never below 10 % brake) 40
    Hold drive_abs_hold 0-100 ms 20
    At full brake: Pulse rate drive_abs_dyn_freq 2-33 Hz (Follows brake depth only) 15
    At full brake: Hold drive_abs_dyn_hold 0-100 ms, 0 = keep the hold above (Follows brake depth only) 0
    With steering: Extra pulse rate drive_abs_corner_freq 0-10 Hz added at full lock, 0 = off 0
    With steering: Hold drive_abs_corner_hold 0-100 ms at full lock, 0 = off 0

    Traction control¶

    Panel CLI drive_tc_mode What it does
    Off OFF (default) The throttle goes to the ESC as you give it
    Ramp RAMP The forward throttle rises no faster than zero to full in the ramp time

    With Ramp, letting off is instant. After a brake or a let-off the throttle rises again from where it is, not from where it was. Traction control never touches the brake or reverse, nor a command above neutral that the ESC uses to brake a car still rolling backwards.

    Turning traction control on, or switching DRIVE AIDS OFF back off, while the trigger is held never cuts the throttle: while traction control or the aids are off, its limit follows the throttle.

    Panel CLI Range Default
    Mode drive_tc_mode OFF, RAMP OFF
    Ramp time drive_tc_ramp_time 50-2000 ms, zero to full throttle 500

    A longer ramp time is gentler: try longer on a slippery surface, shorter on high grip.

    Launch control¶

    Panel CLI drive_lc_mode What it does
    Off OFF (default) No launch control
    LAUNCH switch SWITCH The LAUNCH mode stages a launch; the first forward throttle starts it

    Put LAUNCH on a switch on the Modes tab. Then, for each launch:

    1. At the line, with the throttle at neutral or on the brake, turn LAUNCH on. The launch is staged. The brake, and ABS, work as usual while you wait.
    2. Pull the trigger. The throttle sent to the ESC is at most the start throttle at once, and the limit rises to full over the ramp time. A throttle below the limit goes through as you give it.
    3. The launch ends at the end of the ramp, when you let off to neutral or brake, or when you turn LAUNCH off.

    A launch runs once per LAUNCH activation: lifting in the middle of the run never caps the throttle again. Turn LAUNCH off and on for the next one. Turning LAUNCH on with the throttle already forward stages nothing, so flicking the switch while driving can't cut the throttle. Arming with LAUNCH already on stages a launch.

    A command above neutral that the ESC uses to brake a car still rolling backwards never starts a launch. Traction control stands aside while a launch runs and carries on from the throttle the launch reached, so the handover never cuts the throttle.

    Panel CLI Range Default
    Mode drive_lc_mode OFF, SWITCH OFF
    Start throttle drive_lc_throttle 10-100 % 50
    Ramp time drive_lc_ramp_time 100-5000 ms, start throttle to full 1000

    Launch control uses the throttle and time only. Start with the defaults, lower the start throttle if the car still spins up off the line, and lengthen the ramp if it spins up partway through.

    Checking it¶

    The Drive aids panel's Live box shows whether the aids are on, what ABS is doing (Idle, Braking past the trigger, Releasing the brake), what traction control is doing (Idle, On while driving forward, Holding the throttle back), what launch control is doing (Idle, Staged, Launching), and the throttle from the transmitter next to the throttle sent to the ESC.

    For logs, set debug_mode = DRIVE_AIDS. The debug fields are:

    Field Value
    0 Throttle from the transmitter, x1000
    1 Throttle to the ESC, x1000
    2 Flags: bit 0 aids on, bit 1 ABS braking past the trigger, bit 2 ABS releasing, bit 3 traction control on while driving forward, bit 4 traction control holding the throttle back, bit 5 launch staged, bit 6 launching
    3 While braking with ABS: pulse rate x10. While driving forward with traction control or launching: the throttle allowed, x1000
    4 ABS release brake share, x1000
    5 ABS hold, ms
    6 ABS position in the pulse cycle, ms
    7 Steering wheel, x1000

    Blackbox logs also carry the settings of the profile in use in the drive_abs, drive_tc and drive_lc header lines.

    Source: src/main/flight/drive_aids.c in the firmware.
