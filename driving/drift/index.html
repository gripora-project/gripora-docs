    DRIFT¶

    DRIFT is a mode for drifting. While it is on, the steering wheel steers the wheels directly, as with GYRO OFF, and the gyro only catches the car when it rotates too fast: it adds counter-steer against the turn rate beyond an allowance. There is no heading hold.

    The normal gyro loop treats the steering wheel as a turn-rate request and steers the wheels to get that rate. In a drift that is the wrong way round: the car rotates one way while the front wheels point the other way (counter-steer), so you need to set the wheel angle yourself. DRIFT gives you the wheel angle, and keeps the gyro for what it is good at in a drift: holding the slip angle and stopping a spin.

    What it does¶

    Normal DRIFT GYRO OFF
    Steering wheel A turn-rate request, through the rates and expo Straight to the servo: full stick = full servo travel Straight to the servo
    Gyro Steers the car to the requested turn rate Counter-steers only the turn rate beyond Allowed turn rate None
    Steering I-term (heading hold) Yes Held at zero Held
    Steering D-term Damps as set in the profile Damps as set in the profile None
    Throttle Through the drive aids Unchanged: through the drive aids Unchanged: through the drive aids

    The counter-steer grows in a straight line with the turn rate past the allowance:

    • Counter-steer gain 100 % gives full lock 360 °/s past the allowance. 200 % gets there twice as soon (180 °/s past it); 0 % turns the counter-steer off, and DRIFT then steers like GYRO OFF (the D-term still damps, if the profile has any).
    • Counter-steer limit caps it, as a share of full lock.

    With the defaults (gain 100 %, allowed rate 30 °/s, limit 100 %) the gyro leaves the car alone up to 30 °/s, and reaches full counter-lock at 390 °/s. With gain 150 % and limit 60 % it starts at 30 °/s and reaches its 60 % cap at 174 °/s. The Configurator's Drift panel shows these two rates for the values you enter.

    The counter-steer is added to the wheel angle you give, so if you already hold full counter-lock the gyro can't add more: the total is held to full servo travel.

    Setting it up¶

    1. Set the steering throw first. In DRIFT, as in GYRO OFF, full steering is full servo travel. Set the end points with GYRO OFF on, as described in Setting the steering throw.
    2. Put DRIFT on a switch, on the Modes tab or in the setup wizard's Mode switches step. A switch you can reach while driving is best; a 3-position switch can carry DRIFT and GYRO OFF together.
    3. Check the settings on the Profiles tab, in the Drift panel, on the Ethos Lua suite's Drift page (Tuning → Steering), on the EdgeTX tool's Drift page, or in the CLI (drift_gain, drift_allow, drift_limit). They belong to the PID profile, so a drift profile and a grip profile can each have their own.
    4. Bench check, disarmed: with DRIFT on, the wheels follow the steering wheel. Turn the car quickly by hand around its vertical axis: past the allowed rate the wheels steer against the turn. (Armed with an RPM source, the counter-steer is faded out at a standstill, see below, so check it disarmed.)

    Switch DRIFT on or off on a straight. The steering changes from the stabilised command to the raw wheel angle (or back) at once, so switching in mid-corner moves the wheels.

    Tuning¶

    Start from the defaults and change one value at a time.

    What you see Try
    The gyro fights the drift and pulls the car straight Raise Allowed turn rate
    The car spins out before the gyro catches it Lower Allowed turn rate, or raise Counter-steer gain
    The counter-steer snaps the car straight, or swaps the drift to the other side Lower Counter-steer limit, then the gain
    The steering shakes in a drift Lower the gain; check the steering D-term on the Profiles tab is not too high

    The allowed rate depends on the car and the surface: a car that drifts at a large angle on a polished floor rotates faster than one on carpet. A Blackbox log of a few drifts with DRIFT off shows the turn rates you actually use; set the allowance a little above the steady rate of a good drift.

    The setup wizard's Drift driving style is a different thing: a set of rates (300 °/s, 60 % expo) for the normal gyro loop. It does not turn on the DRIFT mode.

    Interactions¶

    • GYRO OFF wins. With both on, the steering is GYRO OFF: no gyro at all.
    • Failsafe wins. While failsafe is active, DRIFT is ignored and the normal gyro loop steers, so a switch left on when the link dropped doesn't matter. See Failsafe.
    • Drive state. The counter-steer and the D-term are scaled by the drive state's gyro correction, as the normal loop's P and D are: none at a standstill (with an RPM source), reduced in reverse, and faded back in after a direction change without RPM. In reverse the gyro is negated, so the counter-steer still works against the rotation. See Drive State.
    • Gain attenuation. The throttle and speed attenuation on the Profiles tab scale the counter-steer and D-term. The wheel angle you give is never scaled. The profile's P gain and Master Gain don't change the counter-steer: Counter-steer gain alone sets it.
    • Drive aids. DRIFT doesn't touch the throttle: ABS, traction control and launch control work as usual. Use DRIVE AIDS OFF to turn them off.
    • Mixer input limits apply. Unlike GYRO OFF, DRIFT goes through the stabilised steering input, so a min or max set on it with the CLI mixer input command still limits the steering.
    • No heading hold. The steering I-term is held at zero while DRIFT is on, so leaving DRIFT starts the normal loop clean, without a stored correction.
    • PID mode 0 (feedforward only, no gyro) has no gyro to counter-steer with; DRIFT does nothing there.

    Settings¶

    Setting Configurator Ethos EdgeTX Default Range
    drift_gain Counter-steer gain Counter Gain Counter gain 100 % 0-200 %
    drift_allow Allowed turn rate Allowed Rate Allowed rate 30 °/s 0-720 °/s
    drift_limit Counter-steer limit Counter Limit Counter limit 100 % 10-100 % of full lock

    DRIFT is mode id 68. The settings of the profile in use are recorded in the Blackbox log header as drift (gain, allowed rate, limit).

    Racing rules

    DRIFT uses the gyro. Check the rules of your class before running it at a race.
