    Failsafe¶

    The Failsafe tab sets how the controller detects a lost radio link, what value each channel takes once it has, and what the car does if the link stays down.

    Set up and bench-test failsafe before driving: with the wheels off the ground, turn the transmitter off and check that the controller enters failsafe and does what you expect.

    Note

    If a Backup RX Input is configured, losing the main link doesn't reach any of the stages below on its own: control switches to the backup receiver instead. The staged failsafe on this page only takes over if the main link and every backup link are down.

    Pulse Width Limit¶

    Minimum Pulse Width and Maximum Pulse Width (default 885-2115 µs) set the valid channel range. A channel outside it counts as failed, which feeds the Channel Fallback below and the link-loss detection.

    Channel Fallback¶

    What each channel does once it is judged failed:

    • Auto sets a safe value: center for the steering, and neutral for the throttle. Auto is only offered for the steering and throttle channels (and the unused roll and pitch slots).
    • Hold keeps the channel at its last good value. This is the default for every channel after the control channels, so an arm or mode switch that was on stays on.
    • Set sets the channel to a value you choose, in µs.

    The Configurator's help text says Auto sets the throttle "low". On a car the firmware sets it to neutral (the Channel Center from the Receiver tab), so the ESC neither drives nor brakes.

    What happens when the link is lost¶

    1. Detects the loss. No valid frame for about 100 ms, a receiver failsafe flag, or a control channel outside the Pulse Width Limit counts as a bad signal. Arming is blocked from here on until the link is back.
    2. Holds for 300 ms. Each channel keeps its last good value.
    3. Applies Channel Fallback (stage 1). After 300 ms each channel takes its fallback value: with the defaults, steering to center and throttle to neutral, aux channels held. The controller keeps steering with the gyro at this point.
    4. Runs the Stage 2 procedure if the link is still down after the guard time.

    If the car was armed but the throttle had been at neutral for at least Failsafe Throttle Low Delay when the link was lost, it skips the procedure and disarms at once instead. This catches a car that was left armed on the bench, or a transmitter switched off after the car stopped.

    Stage 2 - Failsafe Procedure¶

    Once the guard time (Guard time for stage 2 activation, failsafe_delay, 0.1 s units, default 1.5 s) has passed with the link still down:

    • Drop (the default, DROP in the CLI) disarms the car at once, which stops the motor.
    • Stop (AUTO-LAND in the CLI) sends the Failsafe throttle (failsafe_throttle, default 1500 µs = neutral) to the ESC for Delay for turning off the Motors (failsafe_off_delay, 0.1 s units, default 1 s), then disarms.

    While a failsafe is active:

    • The throttle is the Failsafe throttle, not the throttle channel's fallback value, and the drive aids don't shape it. Leave it at 1500 (neutral) unless you have a reason not to; a value below neutral brakes (and on some ESCs reverses).
    • GYRO OFF is ignored, so the gyro keeps steering even if the GYRO OFF switch was on when the link went.
    • Once the link returns, the controller needs Failsafe Recovery Delay (failsafe_recovery_delay) of good signal before you can arm again.

    Older configurations may still hold failsafe_procedure 2 (GPS Rescue), which the Configurator then shows as GPS Rescue. GPS Rescue no longer exists; choose Drop or Stop.

    Setting up a safe failsafe¶

    The receiver's own failsafe is still your first line of defence. It acts faster (no 100 ms detection) and doesn't depend on the controller:

    1. On your receiver or transmitter, set failsafe to steering centered and throttle at neutral, or to "no pulses" so the controller detects the loss.
    2. Aux channels default to Hold on the controller side. To make a mode switch fall back to a known position, set that channel to Set with the value you want, or in the CLI:
    rxfail 4 s 1900

    rxfail <channel> <a|h|s> [value], with the channel counted from 0. a is Auto (control channels only), h is Hold and s is Set. 3. Pick the Stage 2 procedure and check the Failsafe throttle is neutral. 4. Test it: wheels off the ground, turn the transmitter off, and check the wheels and the steering for the procedure you use. A receiver that keeps sending normal-looking frames after the link is lost can't be detected by the controller.

    Failsafe Test Switch¶

    The FAILSAFE mode on the Modes tab triggers failsafe from a switch, to bench-test your settings or as a panic switch. Failsafe Switch Action picks what it does:

    • Stage 1 treats the control channels as invalid, as on a real signal loss: after the 300 ms hold and the guard time it runs the Stage 2 procedure.
    • Stage 2 skips stage 1 and runs the procedure at once.
    • Kill disarms at once.
