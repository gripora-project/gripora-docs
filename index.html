    Gripora¶

    Gripora is open-source stability-control firmware for RC cars -- a gyro controller that keeps the car pointed where you steer it -- along with the Gripora Configurator used to set it up.

    What it does today:

    • Steering stabilization. A turn-rate (yaw-rate) gyro loop on the steering servo holds the car on the line you steer and catches slides. It corrects the right way in reverse and fades out near a standstill (Drive State), and can be switched off from the transmitter (GYRO OFF).
    • Drive aids. Timed ABS and traction control shape the brake and throttle you give, per PID profile (Drive Aids).
    • A car setup out of the box. Steering on S1, a reversible ESC on M1, optional rear steering and a second ESC, and the car radio channel order (Car Mixer and Channels).

    Launch control and sensor-based aids are planned.

    Gripora builds on the work of WingFlight, a fixed-wing flight controller, which is itself a fork of Rotorflight, which in turn derives from Betaflight. It keeps their MSP protocol, CLI, and configuration philosophy; the aircraft features have been removed.

    Early-stage project

    Gripora is under active development. Firmware is currently distributed as numbered snapshots (0.0.x) rather than stable releases, and both the firmware and this documentation are evolving quickly. Some screenshots and a few labels in the apps still show the fixed-wing software Gripora was forked from. If something here is wrong or missing, please open an issue or a PR.

    Where to start¶

    • New to Gripora? Start with Getting Started and Set Up Your Car.
    • Looking for a Configurator tab? See the Configurator section, organized tab by tab.
    • Want to understand what the controller does on the car (reverse, low speed, ABS, traction control, GYRO OFF)? See Driving Modes & Features.
    • Setting up from the radio? See the Ethos Lua Suite or the EdgeTX Lua Scripts.
    • Looking for CLI settings or MSP details? See Reference.
    • Want to build the firmware or configurator yourself, or contribute? See Contributing.

    Getting help¶

    Open an issue on GitHub for setup help, tuning advice, and project discussion.
