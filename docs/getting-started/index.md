# Getting Started

This section walks through going from a blank controller to a configured car
that is ready for its first run.

1. [Install the Configurator](installing-the-configurator.md) -- either as a
   desktop app, or use the web version at
   [cfg.gripora.org](https://cfg.gripora.org) with no install required.
2. [Flash the firmware](flashing-the-firmware.md) onto your controller.
3. [Make a first connection](first-connection.md) and confirm the sensors
   and receiver are working.
4. Read [Safety](safety.md) before you power the car with the wheels on the
   ground, and set up your receiver's failsafe.
5. [Set up your car](car-setup.md): wire the receiver, steering servo and
   ESC, then run the Setup Wizard for the receiver, model, servos, ESC,
   gyro direction and mode switches.
6. Once you have a working setup, take a [backup](backup-and-restore.md)
   before you start tuning.

To understand what the controller does on the car (the steering gyro in
reverse and at low speed, ABS and traction control), see
[Driving Modes & Features](../driving/index.md).

If you get stuck at any point, the [issue tracker](https://github.com/gripora-project/gripora-firmware/issues)
is the place to ask for help.
